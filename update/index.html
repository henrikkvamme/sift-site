Update Sift
  1. 1

    Replace the old files.

    Open sift-beta.zip and extract its contents.

    Copy its contents into your existing Sift folder. Replace the old files.

    Keep the same folder. Don’t remove Sift from Chrome.

    ZIP
  2. 2

    Reload Sift.

    Open your extensions and click Reload on Sift’s card.

  3. 3

    Refresh YouTube.

    Finish your conversation, then refresh your open YouTube tabs.

    Your settings, sign-in and saved summaries stay.

Where is my Sift folder?

Open Sift’s Details on the Extensions page. With Developer mode on, look for the path beside Loaded from.

Can’t see Reload?

Turn on Developer mode in the top right of the Extensions page.

Installed from the Chrome Web Store?

Chrome updates that version automatically. You don’t need this ZIP.
