How do I start?

Install Sift, then activate Pro with Google in Settings. Your six months start at activation. First 1,000 people; one offer per person.

What does Pro include?

400 summaries and chat answers combined each calendar month, shared across your devices. Plus new Pro features during your offer.

What happens after six months?

Stay free or choose a paid plan. Pricing will be shared at least 30 days before your offer ends. No automatic charges.

Can I use Sift on my phone?

Desktop Chrome for now. Safari on iPhone, iPad, and Mac is next, for websites only.
