Sift

Privacy

Sift's focus features run locally. Hosted AI transfers content only after you choose to use it.

What Sift handles

Sift reads YouTube page content and the current YouTube URL on your device to determine which interface to show and to provide focused search results, a video brief, and transcripts provided by YouTube. It also measures feature use and observed playback in hourly totals. Sift does not store the URL.

What Sift stores

Settings, temporary pass state, and hourly usage totals stay in your browser's local or session storage. Completed generic video summaries and their video IDs are cached in a local database so they can be reused across tabs and browser sessions. Sift does not persist searches, URLs, transcripts, chat questions, or adult-site activity.

Hosted AI

After you enable AI answers, hovering or keyboard-focusing a video requests a summary. Opening the video reuses its cached summary. For a summary or chat answer, Sift sends the selected video's transcript, your question, and recent in-memory chat context to the Sift API and OpenRouter. The free tier selects from suitable free models available at the time. Pro uses a paid model. Sift uses OpenRouter's zero-data-retention routing and denies providers that collect prompt data. The Sift API does not save transcript, question, chat, or answer content after the request finishes. Completed generic summaries are saved only in your browser as described above.

The Sift API stores an opaque installation ID, a keyed hash of the installation credential, subscription status, Stripe customer and subscription identifiers, signed webhook event identifiers, and monthly AI-request counts. These records provide access, prevent duplicate billing events, enforce the monthly allowance, and protect the service from abuse.

If you explicitly allow AI feed calibration, Sift also stores one bounded onboarding draft for your authenticated installation. It contains visible titles, channel names, metadata, descriptions, and your ordering or hide choices. It does not contain thumbnails, URLs, or video IDs. This lets Sift restore your choices after a provider, quota, or network error. The draft is deleted when you accept the feed or after 30 days.

Google sign-in

Signing in is optional. To activate Pro, Google shares your name, email address, profile image, and a stable account identifier with Sift. We use these to manage your account and early-access eligibility. We request no access to your YouTube history, subscriptions, or Google Drive.

We store your account, linked installations, activation and expiry dates, and request counts. Essential session cookies keep you signed in for up to seven days; session records may include your IP address and browser information for security. Provider tokens are encrypted. Activation links expire after ten minutes and work once.

Payments

Stripe processes Sift Pro checkout details such as email, billing country, tax information, and payment method under Stripe's privacy policy. Sift receives customer and subscription identifiers but does not receive or store complete card details.

Diagnostics and feedback

Sift uses PostHog in the EU for anonymous feature counts, error reports, and masked session replay. Recordings show Sift’s layout and interactions, not native YouTube, search text, video descriptions, transcripts, chat content, images, or account details. Raw console logs and network payloads are not recorded. Turn this off with Share anonymous diagnostics in Settings.

Feedback you submit is saved privately and delivered to our GitHub issue tracker. You can include an anonymous diagnostic session link to help us investigate. Your feedback text is shared only when you press Send feedback. The temporary delivery copy is removed after GitHub accepts it.

Sharing and sale

Sift shares hosted-AI content only with the Sift API, OpenRouter, and an eligible zero-data-retention model provider to return the answer you requested. Stripe receives payment and subscription data. Sift does not sell data, use data for advertising, or use data for lending or credit decisions.

Control and retention

Usage totals are retained for the period selected in Settings. You can export the totals or clear local data, including settings, measurements, and cached summaries, at any time. Cached summaries remain until you clear local data or uninstall Sift. Uninstalling Sift also removes its browser storage. High Signal onboarding drafts expire after 30 days and are deleted when you accept the feed.

Use Disconnect this browser in Settings to remove the installation and its credential. Older versions call this Delete hosted account. Any paid subscription for that installation is canceled immediately. Your Google account, offer dates, and account-wide usage counts remain, so reconnecting does not restart an offer or quota. To delete your Sift account and linked data, email support@sifttheweb.com. Stripe and Sift may retain transaction records where law requires it.

Permissions

Sift runs only on youtube.com. It uses storage for local settings, totals, and cached summaries, alarms to end temporary passes, and declarative network rules to redirect distracting YouTube feeds to its packaged search page.

Limited Use

Sift's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Changes and support

Material policy changes will be posted here with a new effective date. For privacy questions or support, email support@sifttheweb.com.
