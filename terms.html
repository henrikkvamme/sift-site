Plain terms for Sift and early access.

Free AI access

Each installation includes 50 AI requests per calendar month. A video summary and each follow-up chat answer count as one request. Sift selects from suitable free models currently available through OpenRouter, so model quality, speed, and availability can vary. Unused requests do not roll over.

Early access

The first 1,000 accounts to activate receive six calendar months of Sift Pro free, starting on activation. One offer per person. Google sign-in is required to claim and link the offer. Installing alone does not reserve a place. No payment card is required.

Pro early access includes 400 summaries and chat answers combined per calendar month, shared across linked installations. Failed requests do not count, and reusing a locally cached summary does not use another request. Unused allowance does not roll over. Reinstalling or reconnecting does not restart the offer.

At the end, you can choose a paid plan or keep using Sift for free. We will announce pricing at least 30 days before your offer ends. Early access does not convert automatically to a paid subscription.

Existing subscriptions

Any existing paid subscription keeps the terms agreed at checkout. Manage or cancel it in Sift Settings. Disconnecting that browser cancels its paid subscription immediately.
